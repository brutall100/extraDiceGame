How to play

The rules

  1. Press Pick who starts. The game chooses Player 1 or Player 2 at random.
  2. Players take turns pressing Roll the dice.
  3. This dice has six faces. Each one is equally likely:
  • +3
  • +4
  • +5
  • +6
  • +10Bonus
  • −10Ouch

The first player to reach 20 points wins. Your score can drop below zero, so the game is never over until it is over.
